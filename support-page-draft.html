Guesstation Support

Guesstation turns your due date into a personal prediction. Built on published CDC birth data and a statistical model that runs entirely on your phone, it estimates your most likely delivery date — and shows how it shifts, week by week.

  • Personalized prediction from your due date or last period
  • Two scenarios: waiting for labor to start on its own, or all deliveries
  • Encouraging weekly affirmations
  • Everything stays on your phone — no account, no tracking, no data collected

Contact

Questions or feedback? Email guesstation.support@gmail.com.

Important

Guesstation is an educational tool, not medical advice. Its estimates come from population statistics; every pregnancy is different, and your provider is the right source for decisions about your care.

Privacy

Guesstation collects no data. All selections persist on your device only; nothing leaves your phone.
